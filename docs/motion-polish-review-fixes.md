## Changes

1. **Language QC and digits.** Template-owned text is marked `data-qc="chrome"`.
   Language QC checks that text, including temporarily hidden chrome. Supplied
   content may include Latin brands/models; visible text overflow is still checked
   on everything. Reviewed vendored content sinks have `data-qc="content"`, and
   the rest of their UI remains chrome. Job-local adapters localize fixed English
   UI labels and avatar initials; vendored bytes and integrity pins are unchanged.
   The standalone scan now mounts templates instead of inspecting inert markup.
2. **Display rule.** A whitespace token with an ASCII Latin letter retains its
   digits, as does the next token: `A52`, `5G`, `iPhone 15`. Other numeric tokens
   use Persian digits and numeric percent/decimal punctuation. For example,
   `کفش Nike Air سایز 42` displays `کفش Nike Air سایز ۴۲`.
   Normalization is at template boundaries; source caption spelling/timestamps
   are preserved. Latin title runs stay in isolated units, keeping `Nike Air`
   and `iPhone 15` in source order under RTL layout. The tracked motion skill
   documents this rule; Python/JavaScript parity and real browser regressions cover it.
3. **Remotion look and motion.** `classic` is again the default for version 2
   as well as version 1; `REMOTION_LOOK` remains authoritative. Each render writes
   `<output>.render-evidence.json` with composition, actual look and delivery size.
   Later scenes keep the product visible during the nine-frame wipe, push from
   1.0 to 1.06 by its end, and raise the headline 16px from opacity 0.6 to 1.
   The HyperFrames/cinematic photo hold stays capped at 1.12. Classic retains its
   simpler layout and title treatment, without adding cinematic caption/effect work.
4. **Comparison admission.** Both short and continuous beat builders resolve
   comparisons with fewer than two non-empty labels to `kinetic_type` and append
   the reason to `why`. An explicitly split `یا` / `در برابر` title can supply
   two labels. The markup fallback also avoids empty cards for stored beats.
